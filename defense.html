Tri-Partite National Defense Blueprint & Remediation Strategy

A coordinated multi-tier framework converting threat intelligence into concrete defensive actions across National Cyber Emergency Response Teams (CERT), Key Point Installation (KPI) Operators, and Law Enforcement Agencies (LEA).

Zero Trust Architecture
🛡️

National CERT / BGD e-GOV CIRT

National Policy, Incident Coordination & Regulatory Audits

  • 1
    Mandatory Enterprise Active Directory Resets

    Enforce immediate domain credential resets across all government agencies, coupled with mandatory FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware tokens for all domain administrative accounts.

  • 2
    Hardening National Verification APIs

    Deprecate unauthenticated National ID and voter lookup endpoints. Enforce zero-knowledge tokenized verification frameworks for banking and telecom partners.

  • 3
    Mandatory Supply-Chain Contractor Audits

    Enact legally binding SAST/DAST source code security audits for all private IT contractors (e.g., Flora Limited) delivering software to state agencies.

  • 4
    Centralized CTI Darknet Early-Warning Feed

    Establish automated real-time monitoring of Russian/Chinese/Tor forums to instantly flag leaks involving .gov.bd, .mil.bd, and core financial institutions.

🏭

Key Point Installation (KPI) Operators

Critical Infrastructure, Banks, Utilities & Telcos

  • 1
    Physical Air-Gapping & Micro-Segmentation

    Completely isolate SCADA/ICS utility networks, core banking databases, and signaling backbones from general corporate office networks.

  • 2
    100% Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)

    Deploy enterprise EDR agents on all servers and administrative workstations with automated ransomware behavioral heuristics enabled.

  • 3
    Immutable Offline Backups (3-2-1 Strategy)

    Maintain cryptographically signed offline and WORM (Write Once Read Many) backups physically disconnected from the corporate active directory.

  • 4
    Strict API Gateways & Rate-Limiting

    Enforce mutual TLS (mTLS) and granular token-bucket rate limits on all partner integration endpoints to prevent mass data scraping.

⚖️

Law Enforcement & Regulatory Bodies

Cyber Police, CID, NTMC, BTRC & Bangladesh Bank

  • 1
    Rapid Incident Notification Hotline

    Create a classified, 24/7 technical alert pipeline linking law enforcement cyber units with critical infrastructure security operations teams.

  • 2
    Victim Citizen Identity Protection Protocol

    Provide an emergency flag system enabling citizens whose full NID, family tree, and facial portraits are leaked to lock digital SIM and bank accounts.

  • 3
    Cross-Border Darknet Takedown Requests

    Collaborate with Interpol and international cyber agencies to disrupt ransomware syndicates (LockBit, RansomHub, Babuk) auctioning sovereign data.

  • 4
    Financial Penalties for Gross Security Negligence

    Enforce statutory penalties for organizations failing to implement baseline cryptographic protections or concealing significant citizen data breaches.
