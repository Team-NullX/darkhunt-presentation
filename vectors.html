National Attack Vectors: How Adversaries Exploit Bangladesh

Technical playbooks illustrating how threat actors weaponize these leaks against national infrastructure and citizens

🏛️ 1. Sovereign Wire Fraud & Forgery

TARGETS: Armed Forces Division, Navy, Police CID
Adversary Misuse Playbook:

Attackers extract authentic military seals, officer signatures, and official dispatch numbers to forge equipment purchase orders and redirect state treasury wires to offshore accounts.

🛡️ Fix: Cryptographic PKI digital signing & multi-party out-of-band wire verification.

⚡ 2. Infrastructure & SCADA Blackout

TARGETS: Flora Limited (Govt IT), tdotnet (BGP), Confidence
Adversary Misuse Playbook:

Adversaries compromise Flora IT developer credentials to poison ministry software updates, and hijack tdotnet BGP gateway routing to knock out power grid monitoring and banking.

🛡️ Fix: Air-gap power SCADA networks; enforce RPKI route validation on all telco gateways.

🎯 3. Corporate Banking Spear-Phishing

TARGETS: The City Bank, Bank Asia, Agrani Bank PLC
Adversary Misuse Playbook:

Ransomware cartels study leaked corporate credit audits to craft hyper-contextual spear-phishing emails to branch managers, deploying ransomware that paralyzes core banking servers.

🛡️ Fix: FIDO2 hardware MFA for all bank managers; immutable air-gapped off-site backups.

📱 4. Mass SIM-Swap & Wallet Hijacking

TARGETS: ECS / NID (120M), Shwapno (410 GB), RabbitBD
Adversary Misuse Playbook:

Syndicates match 120M citizen NID details with retail customer mobile numbers, bypassing telco verification to execute SIM swaps, intercept SMS OTPs, and loot bKash/Nagad accounts.

🛡️ Fix: Eliminate SMS OTP for PIN resets; mandate biometric liveness scan for duplicate SIMs.

✈️ 5. VIP Surveillance & Border Evasion

TARGETS: Biman Bangladesh, NovoAir, Malindo Air
Adversary Misuse Playbook:

Foreign intelligence agencies track flight itineraries, passport numbers, and seat assignments of defense officials and diplomats abroad, while criminals clone booking records to evade watchlists.

🛡️ Fix: End-to-end tokenization of airline PNR data; segregated VIP travel channels.

💀 6. Darknet Double Extortion Pipeline

TARGETS: All 20 Targets Across 16 Tor Hidden Services
Adversary Misuse Playbook:

Initial access brokers sell network credentials; ransomware gangs exfiltrate databases and publish countdown auctions on Tor leak blogs, creating permanent darknet exposure.

🛡️ Fix: Proactive automated darknet CTI scraping; mandatory 72-hour breach disclosure laws.
